PIXEL · ROUND 02

Round 2 takes the 16 × 16 pixel raven further: 14 variants built from the same bitmap, three custom pixel wordmarks drawn on the logo's grid, and 23 typefaces tested as the wordmark.

Variants

$ ls brand/logo/pixelOne bitmap, many treatments
01BASE 16

The 16 × 16 from round 1. Favicon, menu bar, inline UI.

02MICRO 12

Redrawn for 12 px, so it doesn't turn to mush in tight UI.

03DETAIL 24

More room for the hooked bill and a longer run of hackles.

04OUTLINE

1-unit line at 2× resolution. Lighter, more technical.

05OUTLINE 24

The 24 px drawing as a line, for large display use.

06BADGE

Knocked out of a tile with stepped pixel corners. Avatars, app icons, stickers.

07LED

Pixels as dots on a dim matrix, matching the Bitcount and Doto type.

08SCANLINE

Each row as a thin bar, like an old CRT. Works well animated.

09SHADED

Lit from above in the cube's tones: white top, grey side, dark throat.

10DITHER

The same shading in 1-bit, using a Bayer dither at 2×.

11EXTRUDED

Pixels pushed into 3D, the same move as the Figment cube.

12PAIR

Huginn and Muninn back to back. For an org mark or a sibling project.

13STATES
IDLE
BLINK
CAW

Expression frames on the same grid: blink for idle or loading, caw for errors and notifications.

14VIEWFINDER

Inside the Figment corner brackets, so it reads as a sibling of the Figment Engineering mark.

Notes

For discussion
MY PICKCustom Block wordmark with 01 Base 16. Mark and word share one pixel grid and stroke weight, so the lockup scales cleanly in whole-pixel steps.
FONT ROUTEIf you'd rather use an off-the-shelf font: Geist Pixel for something crisp and neutral, or Doto paired with the 07 LED mark for a dot-matrix family that matches the site's hero.
SYSTEMUse 02 Micro 12 for tiny UI, 01 Base 16 by default, and 03 Detail 24 for display. 09 Shaded and 11 Extruded are hero treatments. 13 States can serve as loading and error icons.
